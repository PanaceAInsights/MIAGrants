Method and sources

How this was built, what could not be verified, and which widely repeated claims turned out to be wrong.

How entries were verified

Every entry was checked against a primary source where one exists: the funder's own site, a government page, a published fee schedule, an annual report, or a dated press release. Secondary sources were used only where no primary source publishes the figure, and are labelled as such in the entry.

Three rules were applied throughout:

  • No figure was estimated or inferred. Where a fund does not publish its size or cheque range, the entry says "not published" rather than guessing.
  • Absence of evidence was reported as absence of evidence, not as a finding. Several entries note that something could not be confirmed rather than asserting it does not exist.
  • Where a commonly circulated claim turned out to be wrong, the entry says so, because the wrong version is usually the one in circulation.

Corrections to widely repeated claims

These are the ones most likely to mislead an Australian researcher.

Common beliefWhat the sources actually say
LEO Foundation is the natural funder for dermatology researchLEO explicitly excludes skin cancer. Its Application Guide states it does not support projects on melanoma, CTCL, BCC or SCC. Skin cancer models are permitted only as tools to study other aspects of skin biology.
Australian universities do not really patentWrong. Seven of the top ten attorney-represented provisional filers in Australia in 2025 were universities or CSIRO. NewSouth Innovations filed 60, the University of Sydney 45, CSIRO 43. The problem is scale, not absence.
You can join a US colleague's NIH grant as a subawardeeNot since 2025. NOT-OD-25-104 abolished foreign subawards and NOT-OD-25-155 replaced them with a linked-award structure that the US prime must design in from the start.
An Australian company can access US SBIR grants through a US subsidiaryNo. The awardee must be more than 50% owned and controlled by US citizens or permanent residents, traced through to natural persons. A subsidiary of an Australian parent fails the test.
Wellcome Trust is closing to AustraliansAustralia was never eligible as lead applicant. Eligible hosts are the UK, Republic of Ireland or LMICs. The October 2026 change narrows it further. Wellcome Leap, a separate organisation, is genuinely open and the University of Sydney is already a member.
Cochlear holds over 3,000 patentsIts FY2025 annual report says more than 2,300 patents and patent applications worldwide.
CSIRO earned A$430 million from its WiFi patentsCSIRO's own source states "$430 million" with no currency label, and Australian government publications repeat it unlabelled. The currency cannot be resolved from published sources, so do not assert A$ or US$.
MTPConnect, LaunchVic and Breakthrough VictoriaMTPConnect rebranded to AUScelerate in June 2026. LaunchVic and Breakthrough Victoria merged into Innovation Victoria on 2 July 2026.
NHMRC Development Grants are the proof-of-concept routeNot in 2026. The scheme is paused under review and the funding was redirected to Ideas Grants.
IndieBio, Illumina Accelerator and MassChallenge HealthTechAll three have been renamed or restructured. IndieBio is now SOSV SF and SOSV NY. Illumina Accelerator is Illumina Ventures Labs and abandoned the cohort model. MassChallenge HealthTech no longer exists as a standalone brand.

What could not be verified

Listed here rather than hidden, because knowing the limits of a directory is part of using it well.

  • Any provisional-to-grant conversion rate attributed to IP Australia. IP Australia does not publish one. A figure of 25 to 35% circulates online and could not be found in any IP Australia publication.
  • The total lifetime cost of a multi-jurisdiction patent family. Attorney firm estimates disagree substantially, ranging from around US$75,000 to US$150,000 for five countries up to about A$350,000 for a broad family. No official source publishes a figure.
  • A named Australian winner of a CDMRP melanoma award. Foreign eligibility is explicit and long-standing, but no specific Australian awardee could be verified from public award lists.
  • Whether rare melanoma subtypes fall inside the CDMRP Rare Cancers Research Program or are carved out to the dedicated Melanoma Research Program.
  • Whether Cluster 1 (Health) is inside Australia's Horizon Europe association. The European Commission's own announcement names Clusters 4, 5 and 6 only; Australian government sources name health first. The treaty text is unpublished.
  • Whether the EU and Australia agreement has been signed or is provisionally applied. No Council Decision was locatable, whereas the equivalent instrument for Japan exists.
  • Whether Australians can be funded co-applicants on Wellcome Trust grants. Wellcome's site blocks automated access and its published guidance does not resolve the question.
  • Fund sizes and cheque ranges for many corporate venture arms, which routinely do not publish them. Absence of a figure is normal in that segment, not a red flag.
  • Mayo Clinic Platform_Accelerate international eligibility, which is not explicitly addressed on the program page.
  • Current status of several entities, including the ARPA-H hub and spoke network, HCF Catalyst, and internal venture funds at Garvan, Baker and Centenary.

Scope and bias

This directory was compiled by and for an Australian clinician-researcher working on melanoma, skin cancer and AI in cancer detection. That shapes it. Entries are assessed for relevance to that work, so a scheme marked as a poor fit here may be an excellent fit for a different field. Therapeutics-focused readers in particular should note that several entries flagged as low fit for a software or diagnostics product are strong options for a drug development program.

Nothing here is legal, financial or investment advice. The author is not a patent attorney and not a financial adviser. Verify everything with the funder and get your own professional advice before acting.

Corrections

If an entry is wrong or out of date, please say so. A directory like this decays quickly, and the most useful contribution anyone can make is telling the author which entry has gone stale.
