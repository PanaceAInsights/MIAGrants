What closes soon

Everything in this directory that carries a date, ordered with the most urgent first. Rows in red close within roughly fourteen weeks of 15 August 2026.

Re-verify every date directly with the funder before acting. Deadlines move, rounds get pulled, and several schemes here changed status in the twelve months before this was compiled.

Only future dated deadlines are listed. Rolling schemes, paused programs and policy effective dates are excluded and appear further down or on their own tab.

Nothing matches that filter.

Rolling and always-open

Some of the most useful money in this directory has no deadline at all, which is exactly why it gets overlooked. Worth knowing:

  • University of Sydney Pre-Seed Launch Fund, up to A$500,000, EOI accepted year-round with a response in one to two weeks.
  • R&D Tax Incentive, 43.5% refundable. An entitlement, not a competition. If you have a company doing eligible R&D you simply claim it.
  • ARPA-H Mission Office solicitations, four of them, rolling to 5 March 2029.
  • MRFF Frontier, rolling expressions of interest to 31 March 2027, up to A$25 million.
  • USAMRDC Broad Agency Announcement, rolling to 30 September 2027, with no funding ceiling.
  • NVIDIA Inception and Microsoft for Startups, rolling, free, equity-free. About an hour of effort each.
  • Main Sequence Researcher Office Hours and Folklore Office Hours, bookable any time, no commitment.

Compiled August 2026. Dates are as published at the time of compilation. Always confirm with the funder.
